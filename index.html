Subject: Regulatory Data Request for Swiss / EU SDS Adaptation: Acme Industrial Sealant US-400

Hello Regulatory Affairs Team,

We are preparing a Swiss-compliant Safety Data Sheet (ChemV / EU CLP / REACH Annex II) for Acme Industrial Sealant US-400 based on your US OSHA SDS. Most physical, toxicological, and UN transport data can be reused, however four data points are required to finalize classification for Switzerland and the EU:

1. Masked Ingredient (Section 3): Please disclose the CAS/EC number and GHS/CLP hazard classification (or share the raw-material SDS under NDA) for "Proprietary Amine Hardener" (10 to 20 wt%).
2. Narrower Range (CAS 112-24-3): The 1 to 5 wt% range crosses the EU CLP skin corrosion vs. irritation boundary. Please confirm a narrower band (e.g. below 1%, 1 to below 3%, or 3 to below 5%) or mixture pH / skin-test data.
3. REACH 2020/878 and 2023/707 Status: Please confirm whether any ingredient is present at >= 0.1 wt% as PBT, vPvB, PMT, vPvM, or an Endocrine Disruptor.
4. Swiss Storage and VOC Data: Please confirm total VOC weight percentage for Swiss VOCV reporting and any combustibility data for the uncured paste.

Once received, we can finalize the Swiss SDS in DE, FR, and IT promptly.
